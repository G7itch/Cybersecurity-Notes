# Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Cybersecurity a term that refers to the selected tools, technologies, methodologies used to secure digital assets and spaces. The Fortinet NSE 1 course looks at foundational knowledge such as basic terms, principles of IT security, and first line of defence methods.

**Cybersecurity**: Protecting computer networks, devices and information from damage, loss or unauthorised access. This means preserving the CIA triad of data digitally. There are different categories of cybersecurity including but not limited to:

- Critical infrastructure

- Application Security

- Network Security

- IoT Security

- Cloud Security

- People and Process Security

**InfoSec**: The practice of protecting information. Involves prevention, intervention and remediation. Information can include both digital and physical files. All data should be categorised to identify how much protection is required for the data:

- Confidential

- Secret

- Top-secret

- Unrestricted

**Information Systems Security**: Part of InfoSec that aims to secure the systems that store information.

### First Line of Defence

The leading cause of compromise is human error. We should educate users against potential attacks. We should also store backups to aid us in restoring systems after an incident.

### Principles of InfoSec

The CIA triad is crucial in InfoSec: *Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability.* InfoSec works to uphold these principles. The DAD triad is the opposite of the CIA triad and it describes what InfoSec works to protect against: *Disclosure, Alteration, Denial*.

Another crucial framework is the AAA:

- **Authentication**: Makes sure the user is who they say they are.

- **Authorisation**: Makes sure the user is allowed to access what they are requesting.

- **Accounting**: Makes sure a record of the users access is logged.
